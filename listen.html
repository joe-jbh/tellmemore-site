Hear the voices

The app reads the Bible aloud in voices recorded ahead of time, so they sound the same every time and play with the screen locked. English has Bella and Michael. Here are the voices we've made for the Spanish and Chinese Bibles: the same short passage in each, and the whole chapter if you'd like to listen longer.

About these recordings

Each voice is made on our own Mac with Kokoro, an open speech model, from the Bible text the app shows. Nothing is read by a person, and nothing is sent anywhere to make them. In the app, each word lights as it's read; in Spanish and Chinese the lighting is close rather than exact.
