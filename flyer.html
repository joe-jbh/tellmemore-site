Tell Me More

Bible Reader · for iPad and Mac · the reader is free

A Bible that reads to you, listens to you, and answers your questions.

One screen. Big buttons with words on them. Tap Speak and the page is read aloud, each word lit as it's said. Say "Romans 8" and you're there.

Ask about a name, a word or a passage — "Who was Melchizedek?", "What does this mean?" — and get the answer from six reference works, in their own words, with the commentary on the very verse you're reading.

No account, no sign-in. Nothing you read or say leaves the iPad.
